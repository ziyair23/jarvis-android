# L11 — Make your own protocols

## What he does
"Jarvis, make a protocol called gym: phone on quiet, play my workout playlist, lights off."
Later: "gym protocol" / "gym mode" / "run gym" / "start gym".
Also: "what protocols do I have?", "delete the gym protocol".

## What Jarvis does
- Claude turns his description into steps and saves the protocol with the save_protocol tool. Jarvis replies in one short line: "Gym protocol saved: quiet, workout playlist, lights off."
- Running it does each step in order and answers in one short line; a step that fails doesn't stop the others.
- Saved only on the phone. Up to 20 protocols, up to 8 steps each.

## Allowed steps (anything else is refused)
lights, phone_control (quiet, flashlight, volume), quiet_mode, open_app, play_music, media_control, set_timer, set_alarm, remind_me, driving_mode, get_weather, calendar_events, status_report, list_read, house_speaker.
Never: texting, calling, replying, answering/declining calls, tapping or typing on screen, logging or deleting data, memory changes, budgets, another protocol.

## Acceptance tests
1. Saving "gym" with steps phone_control, play_music, lights → saved; reply names it and lists the steps in a few words.
2. Any step with send_text, call, reply_message, screen_tap, screen_type, screen_action, answer_call, decline_call or run_protocol → whole protocol refused with "I can't put texting, calls or screen taps in a protocol, sir." Nothing saved.
3. Names are matched loosely: "Gym", "gym protocol", "gym mode", "the gym protocol" all mean "gym".
4. Built-in names (dash, baby, home, goodnight) can't be overwritten: "That one's built in, sir. Pick another name."
5. 0 steps or more than 8 → refused with a short reason. A 21st protocol → refused until he deletes one.
6. Saying "gym protocol" / "gym mode" / "run gym" runs it instantly on the phone (no trip to Claude) when a custom protocol with that name exists; built-ins still work exactly as before.
7. "what protocols do I have" lists built-ins and his own in one short line.
8. Deleting removes it; deleting a name that doesn't exist says so.
9. Saving a name that already exists replaces it and says "updated".
