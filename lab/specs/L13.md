# L13 — YouTube pulse

## What he does
"Jarvis, how's my channel doing?" / "How's my YouTube doing?" / "How many subscribers do I have?" / "How's my latest video doing?" / "YouTube stats." / "Check my channel." Claude can also call `youtube_stats` for freer wording ("is the teaser picking up?").

## What Jarvis does
- Reads his channel's numbers from the free YouTube Data API (3 quota units per ask, 10,000 free a day) and says one short line:
  "1,240 subscribers, 18 more this week, sir. Newest, Hoodie drop teaser, 900 views in 2 days. Your last five averaged 1,100."
- The change is measured against the numbers saved when he asked before (one snapshot a day, kept 60 days, on the phone): the oldest from the last 8 days ("this week", "since Monday", "since yesterday"), else the newest earlier one ("in 12 days"). First ask ever: no change part.
- Titles for the ear: hashtags, emoji and symbols removed, at most 8 words.
- Hidden subscriber count → "Subscriber count is hidden on the channel, sir." then the video part. No videos → "No videos up yet."
- Setup in Jarvis settings: his channel handle (with or without @, or a channel link) and an optional YouTube key. A blank key falls back to his Google voice key, then his Gemini key (works if YouTube Data API v3 is turned on for that Google Cloud project). Keys are never in the repo.
- Errors, short: no key/handle → "I need a YouTube key and your channel handle in Jarvis settings first, sir."; unknown channel → "I can't find that channel, sir. Check the handle in Jarvis settings."; 400/401/403 → "YouTube won't take the key, sir. Turn on the YouTube Data API for it in Google Cloud."; no network/other → "Can't reach YouTube right now, sir."
- Channel links copied from a tab (/videos, /featured, /shorts) still find his channel; a video link (youtu.be/…) is refused, never looked up as a handle.
- The key goes in a request header, never the URL. A 403 for the daily limit → "YouTube's daily limit is used up, sir. Try again tomorrow."; a key YouTube refuses gets one retry with his next key. No network → the no-signal line at once; 5-second timeouts.
- "subs"/"subscribers" only with "my" ("how are my subs" counts, "how are the subs" doesn't); "the channel" only with doing/looking/going.
- Handled on the phone as a reflex for the exact phrasings (no Claude cost); the exchange is kept in the conversation so "and the one before?" makes sense to Claude.
- Read-only: never posts, comments, uploads or changes anything.

## Acceptance tests
1. Phrasings above (with "Jarvis", "sir", "today", "on YouTube") → handled. Not handled: "open YouTube", "play my YouTube playlist", "how's my day looking", "how's the weather", "how's my youtube music", "how many miles do I have", "check my messages".
2. 1,240 subs now, 1,222 seven days ago; newest "Hoodie drop teaser #pursuit 🔥" 2 days old at 900 views; five before at 1000/1200/800/1400/1100 (a 7th ignored) → exactly the example line.
3. 3 fewer since 18 hours ago → "3 fewer since yesterday"; same count since Monday → "no change since Monday"; 1 subscriber, first ask, newest 5 hours old with 1 view and one older video at 7 → "1 subscriber, sir. Newest, First, 1 view in 5 hours. The one before had 7."
4. Hidden count with a 10-minute-old video at 40 views → "Subscriber count is hidden on the channel, sir. Newest, Clip, 40 views in under an hour."
5. Snapshots: one per day (same day replaced), the comparison picks the oldest within 8 days, else the newest earlier one, none from only today; 60+ days dropped. Save/load round trip survives a broken line.
6. Channel input: "@pursuit.official", "youtube.com/@marcus27?si=abc", "https://youtube.com/channel/UC…" (22 chars) work; "" and "my channel!!" → setup line.
7. Titles: hashtags/emoji removed; 10 words → first 8 + "…".
8. All earlier tests (L8, L17, L20, L26, L27) still pass. Settings screen has the two new fields; nothing else on the screen changes.
