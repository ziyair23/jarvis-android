## Round-1 problems
1. Tab links → stranger's channel: **fixed**. A `youtu.be` link or `/watch` is refused.
2. Key in the URL: **fixed**. The key goes in the `X-Goog-Api-Key` header, no `key=` is left in any URL, and logged errors are scrubbed anyway. No secrets in the repo.
3. Quota/restricted key: **fixed**. `quotaExceeded`/`dailyLimitExceeded` → "YouTube's daily limit is used up, sir. Try again tomorrow."; a refused key gets one retry with the next key (at most 2 keys, 6 quota units).
4. Offline: **fixed**. `Brain.isOnline()` false → "Can't reach YouTube right now, sir." at once; 5 s timeouts.
5. Loose phrasings: **fixed**. "how are the subs" and "how's the channel" are no longer taken.

## Checks as asked
Secrets: none in the repo, not logged. Cost: free API, 3 units per ask (6 at most with the retry) of 10,000 a day. Offline/L8: a reflex, instant no-signal line, never held. Key fallback: YouTube → voice → Gemini, retry once on refusal. Read-only: GET only, can't post. Safe 5.

## Remaining (cosmetic)
A legacy custom URL (`youtube.com/c/Name`) gets the "I need a YouTube key and your channel handle…" line, which is slightly misleading when a key is set. **Fix:** in `pulse`, when `keys` isn't empty but `q == null`, return "Put your @handle in Jarvis settings, sir. That link isn't one I can use."
