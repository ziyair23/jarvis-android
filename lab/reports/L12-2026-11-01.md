## Problems
1. **(Blocker, Works) Brainstorm asks are answered from the vault.** The spec sends "any ideas for the caption" and "what ideas do you have" to Claude: those are him asking Jarvis to come up with ideas. But the same ask with a kind word is caught: "any video ideas for the drop?" → "No video ideas on the drop yet, sir.", and "give me video ideas for the hoodie", "any caption ideas", "list collab ideas" are read from the vault the same way. He asked for help and gets a list lookup. The cause is in `IdeaVault.parse` (FIND): a kind word alone counts as "my vault".
   **Fix (verified: IdeaVaultTest and IdeaVaultIndepTest all pass, 15/15, with the test un-ignored):** change
   `if (my.trim() != "my" && brand.isEmpty() && type.isEmpty() && have.isEmpty()) return null`
   to
   `if (my.trim() != "my" && brand.isEmpty() && have.isEmpty()) return null`.
   "read my video ideas", "what video ideas do I have" and "what Pursuit ideas do I have for hoodies" are still caught. Then remove `@Ignore` from `IdeaVaultIndepTest.brainstormAsksGoToClaude`.
2. (Minor, Feel) "Scratch that idea" right after Claude suggests something deletes the newest *vault* idea, which may be weeks old. It does say what it scratched, but there's no undo. **Fix:** in `handle(DeleteLast)`, when the newest was saved more than 10 minutes ago and he didn't say "my last", reply "Your last saved idea is: …. Say 'delete my last Pursuit idea' to bin it." instead.
