## Problems found, and fixed in build 109
1. **Tapping an agent in the brain opened its page and closed it instantly** (his report: "can't tap my agents").
   - Reproduce: on a touch screen, tap any agent's dot.
   - Cause: a finger tap ends with a "click" a few milliseconds later. By then the backdrop is over the screen, the click lands on it, and the backdrop closes the page. Mouse clicks don't do this, so every earlier test passed.
   - Fix: the backdrop ignores clicks for 600 ms after a page opens. A tap right on a dot always picks the agent, even when the dot is next to Jarvis in the centre.
   - Result: all 13 agents open by finger tap at phone speed. Regression test added.
2. **Agent access also depended entirely on the canvas.**
   - Fix: a row of agent buttons under the brain (shipped in 107), plus a second click route on the canvas.
   - Screen errors now go to the flight log (`dash` lines).
3. **Daily checks lost a day when there was no signal.**
   - Cause: Bills/Appointments, the Supplies price check and Coach marked the day as done before talking to Claude, so a failed call skipped those texts for good.
   - Fix: the day only counts once the check succeeds. Retries wait 30 minutes for Bills/Appointments, a day for Supplies, and the next unlock for Coach.
4. **Driving-app pay could look like a bill** ("payment of $85.20 scheduled for…").
   - Fix: Dasher, Uber Driver, Spark, Amazon Flex, Gridwise and Stride notifications are never treated as bills (unit-tested). Packages already ignored them.
5. **From the build 105 flight log** (fixed in 107, re-checked here):
   - He falsely "confessed" he never sent the Toby reply. He now gets a record of what his actions actually did.
   - He tacked old texts and the battery onto unrelated answers. Stale offers are now dropped.
   - A Claude 400 on a bad thinking-block signature gave a bare "Done, sir." He now retries without the thinking blocks.
   - `open_app` failed on the first try with the wrong field name.
   - "stopp listening" was ignored.
