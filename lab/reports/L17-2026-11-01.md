# L17 Call notes — test report (2026-11-01)

**Final verdict: PASS (round 2).** Round 1: FAIL.

Note: no Agent tool was available in this run, so testing followed the jarvis-tester skill but was run by the lab session itself, from the spec. Not a fully independent test.

**How tested:** no Android SDK, so no Gradle build. `CallNotes.kt` is plain Kotlin; `CallNotesTest` (10 tests) compiled and run with Gradle's bundled Kotlin compiler: all green. Android glue (`CallNotesStore` in Moments.kt, `Messages.announceCall`, `Reflex.handle`) checked by code review: symbols, imports and scope (`line`, `ask`, `unknown`, `spammy`) verified.

## Round 1: FAIL
1. **Noisy car, misheard name.** "note for abby she owes me twenty" with caller "Abbey" → not matched, went to Claude, note lost. Fix: sound-alike fallback (`CallNotes.sound`) in `findWho` and `resolve`.
2. **Unknown number.** After an unknown call, "note that this was the dentist" was saved under the raw number, and the note line is suppressed for unknown numbers, so it could never come back. Fix: only known callers are remembered (`called` moved after the unknown/spam check); "note that" after an unknown call goes to Claude.
3. **Common phrasing missed.** "do I have any notes on Abbey" went to Claude. Fix: added "do i have any / have i got any / got any".

## Scenarios
- 7 AM, car Bluetooth, Abbey calls: "Abbey is calling, sir. Your note: she owes you twenty. Answer it?" then the usual 5 s answer window. Pass.
- Evening, just hung up with a customer for Pursuit: "note that he wants two large hoodies" → "Noted for Marco, sir." Pass.
- At home: lights/Govee reflexes unaffected (call notes only match "note that / note for / notes on"). Pass.
- Noisy car: misheard first name found by sound. Pass (round 2).
- No signal: reflex, works offline. Pass.
- Vague: "note that" with nothing after → goes to Claude. Pass.
- Follow-up within 3 s: window stays open after "Noted for…, sir." A follow-up goes to Claude, not appended to the note. Acceptable.
- Quiet mode: the note rides along in the silent missed-call note. Pass.
- Misfire: nothing sends, posts or buys; notes stay in SharedPreferences, are not added to Claude's conversation history. Safe = 5.

## Minor, not blocking
- Inside a live two-way voice session, "note that…" goes to the live model, not the reflex.
- Reflex's trailing-word trim drops a final "now"/"please" from a note.
- Only incoming calls are remembered as "last caller" (outgoing calls aren't seen by the notification listener).
