# L27 — Where were we?

## What he does
After a break (a delivery, a call, the baby): "Jarvis, where were we?" / "What were we talking about?" / "Where was I?" / "What was I saying?" / "Let's pick up where we left off." / "Where did we leave off?"

## What Jarvis does
- Handled on the phone (reflex): instant, free, works with no signal. Not added to the conversation itself.
- Finds the last real topic in the saved conversation and says it in one or two short lines, his words turned round to "you", plus the first sentence of Jarvis's answer:
  "You'd asked: should you do black or oxblood for the next drop. I said: Oxblood, sir."
- Skips quick phone commands (music, volume, time, lights, car reminders: now marked `q` in the history), read-out messages ("Message from …"), small talk ("thanks", "yeah", "okay do it") and earlier "where were we?" asks.
- More than two hours ago: starts "Earlier today," / "Last night," / "Yesterday," / "On Monday,". Older than 7 days, nothing saved, or after "drop it" → "Nothing on the go, sir."
- No answer saved yet → just "You'd asked: …".
- His question at most 15 words; Jarvis's answer at most 20 words (cut with "…").
- His next line goes to Claude as usual, with the full history, so he can carry straight on.

- History keeps the newest 40 real turns and 10 quick ones, so reflexes can't push the topic out.
- Recogniser capitals are lowered ("What time…" → "what time…"); a topic from the small hours (before 5 AM) asked about that morning is "Last night,".
- A yes to sending a text or reply only counts within 2 minutes of the ask (Brain prompt); older, Jarvis reads it back and asks again.

## Acceptance tests
1. Phrasings above (with "Jarvis", "so", "anyway", "sir") → handled. Not handled: "where were you", "what were we doing yesterday", "where was I last night", "where are we", "what was I paying for gas".
2. History: drop question + answer 20 min ago, then "pause"/"Paused." (quick), a "Message from Jess…" line, "thanks"/"Of course, sir." → "You'd asked: should you do black or oxblood for the next drop. I said: Oxblood, sir."
3. Empty history, only quick turns, or a topic 8 days old → "Nothing on the go, sir."
4. Question with no answer → "You'd asked: what time does the post office close."
5. Yesterday 1 PM caption request, now Wednesday 2 PM → "Yesterday, you'd asked: draft a caption for the hoodie post. I said: Built for the late shift."; 10 PM yesterday asked at 8 AM → "Last night,"; Monday → "On Monday,"; 90 minutes ago → no prefix.
6. A 30-word answer is cut to 20 words with "…".
7. A previous "where were we" and "yeah" after the topic are skipped.
8. Reflex turns are saved with `q: true`; Claude and live turns are not. All earlier tests (L8, L17, L20, L26) still pass.
