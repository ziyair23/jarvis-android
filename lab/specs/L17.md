# L17 — Call notes

## What he does
- Right after a call (within an hour of it ringing): "Jarvis, note that she owes me twenty." 
- Any time: "Jarvis, note for Abbey she wants the black hoodie in large."
- "Jarvis, what are my notes on Abbey?" / "Jarvis, delete my notes on Abbey."

## What Jarvis does
- Saves a one-line note, in his words, tied to the caller, on the phone only (never sent anywhere, never sent to Claude to store).
- Replies "Noted for Abbey, sir."
- The next time that person calls, the ring announcement carries the newest note, turned round to "you": "Abbey is calling, sir. Your note: she owes you twenty. Answer it?" The note goes before "Answer it?".
- "What are my notes on Abbey?" reads the newest three, newest first. "Delete my notes on Abbey" removes them all.
- Handled on the phone (reflex): instant, free, works with no signal.
- If he says "note that …" and nobody has called in the last hour, Jarvis behaves exactly as before (it goes to Claude as usual).
- Unknown numbers and spam calls never get a note line (the spam warning stays as it is).
- Limits: 5 notes per person (oldest dropped), 50 people; a spoken note is cut to 15 words.

## Acceptance tests
1. Abbey's call rang 10 minutes ago → "note that she owes me twenty" → "Noted for Abbey, sir." and the note is saved for Abbey.
2. Next call from Abbey while driving → "Abbey is calling, sir. Your note: she owes you twenty. Answer it?"
3. Same call, not driving → "Abbey is calling, sir. Your note: she owes you twenty."
4. "note that …" with no call in the last hour → not handled (normal Claude path).
5. "note for Abbey she wants the black hoodie in large" with Abbey a known caller → note saved for Abbey with text "she wants the black hoodie in large". Two-word names ("Abbey Smith") are matched by full or first name.
6. "what are my notes on abbey" → newest three, newest first, in one short line; none → "No notes on Abbey, sir."
7. "delete my notes on abbey" → all gone, "Cleared 2 notes on Abbey, sir."
8. 6th note for one person drops the oldest; storage survives a save/load round trip, including notes with tabs, newlines or odd characters.
9. Nothing can text, call, post or send; notes never leave the phone. Unknown/spam calls get no note line.
10. Other reflexes ("drop it", "forget it", music, volume) are unchanged.
