# L8 — No-signal mode

## What he does
Asks Jarvis something while there's no signal (dead spot, basement, phone briefly offline).

## What Jarvis does
- Instead of failing after a long wait, says right away: "No signal, sir. I'll come back to that when it's back." and holds the request (on the phone, in memory).
- Instant on-phone commands (time, music, volume, flashlight, timers, lights on Wi-Fi) keep working as before; only requests that need Claude are held.
- When the phone is back online, Jarvis comes back to what he asked, in one spoken line that starts "Signal's back, sir.":
  - questions get a short answer;
  - anything that asked Jarvis to DO something (text, call, lights, music, timer, reminder…) is never done late — Jarvis just reminds him what he asked and that he can say it again.
- Keeps at most the newest 3 held requests; a repeat counts as the newest; anything older than 45 minutes is dropped.
- If Claude still can't be reached when the signal returns, Jarvis just reminds him what he asked, in his words.
- In quiet mode the catch-up arrives as a silent note instead of out loud.

## Acceptance tests
1. No network at all → the held line is spoken without a network wait; the request is held.
2. Network present but the call fails twice → same held line (not "I can't reach the network").
3. Held actions are answered with NO phone tools available, so nothing can be texted, called, switched or played late.
4. Signal back with a question and an action held → one line, "Signal's back, sir." once, the question answered, the action only mentioned.
5. More than 3 held → only newest 3; held for over 45 minutes → dropped silently.
6. Claude unreachable at catch-up → reminder line in his words.
7. History saves his original words for a held request, not the catch-up instructions.
8. Nothing about normal online behaviour changes.
