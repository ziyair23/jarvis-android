# L12 — Pursuit idea vault

## What he does
- Driving, at the stove, mid-shoot: "Jarvis, Pursuit idea: the silhouette fills with paint on Drop 2." / "New brand idea: oxblood hoodie, cream wordmark." / "Video idea: film the hoodie on the bridge at dusk." / "Idea for Pursuit: chain the drops like TV episodes." / "Save a Pursuit drop idea that …" / "Caption idea …" / "Collab idea for the brand …"
- Later: "What Pursuit ideas do I have for hoodies?" / "Read my video ideas." / "Read my Pursuit ideas." / "How many Pursuit ideas do I have?" / "Scratch that idea." / "Delete my last Pursuit idea."
- Claude gets `pursuit_idea_save` and `pursuit_ideas` (read-only lookup with dates) for freer wording and to build on an idea with him.

## What Jarvis does
- Saves the idea in his own words (capitals, numbers kept; leading ":" and a trailing "sir" dropped), on the phone, with a kind: what he said (design, drop, post/content/caption, video/shoot/TikTok/reel, collab) or guessed from the words (hoodie/tee/logo → design, TikTok/film/reel → video, caption/post/story → post, drop/launch/price → drop, collab/influencer → collab, else idea). Replies "Saved to Pursuit ideas, sir."
- Reads back up to three, his words turned round to "you", at most 15 words each:
  - with a topic: best match first (most matching words, then newest), "3 on hoodies, sir. Best match: … Then: … And: …"; none → "No Pursuit ideas on caps yet, sir."
  - no topic: newest first, "4 Pursuit ideas, sir. Newest: … Then: … And: …"; one → "One video idea, sir: …"; none → "No Pursuit ideas yet, sir." / "No drop ideas yet, sir."
- Plurals match ("hoodies" finds "hoodie", "stories" finds "story").
- Count: "4 Pursuit ideas saved, sir." / "1 video idea saved, sir."
- Scratch: removes the newest, "Scratched: mint diamond cap."
- Not caught (goes to Claude as usual): "any ideas?", "any ideas for the caption", "what ideas do you have", "I have an idea let's go to the park", "idea", "Pursuit idea" with no idea or a one-word one, "what's a good idea for dinner".
- Same idea twice is kept once; at most 300 (oldest dropped). Handled as a reflex: instant, free, works with no signal. Never posts or sends anything.

## Acceptance tests
1. Each save phrasing above → saved text exactly his words after the command; kinds as listed.
2. The "not caught" list → not handled.
3. Kind guessing: "oxblood hoodie with the cream wordmark" → design; "TikTok of the hoodie on the bridge" → video; "price the first drop at 45" → drop; "work in silence, let the results talk" → idea.
4. Four saved (oxblood hoodie, black hoodie with mint diamond, TikTok of my hoodie on the bridge, blank-dot tees) → "what Pursuit ideas do I have for hoodies?" → "3 on hoodies, sir. Best match: TikTok of your hoodie on the bridge at dusk. Then: black hoodie, mint diamond on the sleeve. And: oxblood hoodie with the cream wordmark."; "read my video ideas" → "One video idea, sir: TikTok of your hoodie on the bridge at dusk."; "read my Pursuit ideas" → 4, newest three; counts as above.
5. Two drop ideas → "2 drop ideas, sir. Newest: … Then: …."
6. "Scratch that idea" removes the newest and says it; empty → "No Pursuit ideas yet, sir."
7. Duplicates (any case) kept once; 305 saves → 300 kept, oldest gone; save/load survives newlines and a broken line.
8. Claude lookup returns "- <date> [kind] text" lines, or "No saved Pursuit ideas match."
9. All earlier tests (L8, L13, L17, L20, L26, L27) still pass.
