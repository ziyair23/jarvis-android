   Repro: 8 morning offers $7 / 3 mi / 20 min and 8 evening offers $9 / 2 mi / 54 min → "Your best stretch is 7 AM to 9 AM, about $2.33 a mile and 21 an hour, from 8 offers. Weakest is 5 PM to 7 PM, $4.50 a mile." Marcus hears the "weakest" stretch paying nearly double per mile.
   Fix (verified, all tests pass; gives "Weakest is 5 PM to 7 PM, about 10 an hour."), in `OfferMath.bestHours`:
   ```kotlin
   if (worst != null && worst.score < best.score * 0.75) {
       val hourIsWorse = worst.perHour != null && worst.perHour / minPerHour < worst.perMile / minPerMile
       val why = if (hourIsWorse) "about ${Math.round(worst.perHour!!)} an hour" else "${money(worst.perMile)} a mile"
       sb.append(" Weakest is ${clock(worst.start)} to ${clock(worst.start + 2)}, $why.")
   }
   ```
