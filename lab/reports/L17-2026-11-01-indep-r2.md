## Remaining problem (minor, does not block)
1. **A filler-only note gets saved.** Repro: Abbey rang 5 min ago; "Jarvis, note that, sir." → saved as note "sir" → next ring: "Abbey is calling, sir. Your note: sir." Cause: `TAIL` needs a separator before "sir", so a body of just "sir." keeps "sir".
   **Fix:** in CallNotes.kt change `TAIL` to `Regex("(?:^|[\\s,.!?]+)(?:sir|jarvis|please)?[\\s,.!?]*$", RegexOption.IGNORE_CASE)`, and in `parse` make `ADD_FOR`/`ADD` return null when `body(r)` is empty (ADD already does).
