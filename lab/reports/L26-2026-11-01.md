## Conflicts
Reflex order is call notes → say-that-again → car reminders → existing reflexes; no phrase overlaps (probed). Offline (L8): it's a reflex, so it works with no signal and nothing is held. The Welcome back / Driving mode off lines go through `sayFirst`, so they are recorded for "say that again" (L20), and quiet mode, a phone call or night quiet hours turn them into a notification instead of losing them. The `remind_in_car` tool only stores; held catch-ups (L8) have no tools, so nothing is added late.

## Scenarios
7 AM: gets in, Bluetooth connects → "Welcome back, sir. Roads look clear. Reminder: get gas." Evening shift, mid-delivery: "remind me when I park to grab the bag" → said when the car Bluetooth drops. Home: "remind me when I get home…" goes to the home reminders as before. Noisy car: see problem 1. No signal: works. Vague: "remind me in the car" → Claude. Follow-up within 3 s: same reflex. Misfire check: it only speaks; it never texts, calls, taps or posts (a reminder "text Abbey I'm late" is only read out).

## Problems (none blocking)
1. **Cut-off phrase saves a junk reminder.** "remind me when I get in the car to" (the recogniser stops early) → saved "to" → "Reminder: to." **Fix:** in `CarReminders.add(task, out)` use `replace(Regex("^(to|that|about)( |$)"), "")`. Verified: all 18 car tests pass, plus a cut-off check.
2. **A Bluetooth blip at a red light uses up park reminders.** The car Bluetooth drops for a second → "Driving mode off, sir. Reminder: grab the bag." → the reminder is gone before he actually parks. **Fix:** in `checkCar`, when `!inCar` and park reminders are waiting, delay the line: `main.postDelayed({ if (!DriveMode.on) sayFirst("Driving mode off, sir." + carDue(true)) }, 15_000)`, and leave the line immediate when nothing is due. This also needs a `CarRemindersStore.has(c, out)` check (`edit(c) { l -> l.any { it.out == out } }`).
3. **Car plus a time gets swallowed.** "remind me in the car in an hour to call mom" / "…in the car tomorrow to get gas" → saved as a car reminder "in an hour to call mom". **Fix:** in `add(task, out)`, return null when `Regex("^(in (an?|\\d+|a few) |tomorrow|tonight|at \\d|this (morning|afternoon|evening))").containsMatchIn(t)`, so Claude handles it.
4. Minor: "remind me when I park at home to grab the bag" → "Reminder: at home to grab the bag." Same fix area: strip a leading `at (home|work) (to )?`.
