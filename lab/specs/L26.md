# L26 — Car reminders

## What he does
- "Jarvis, remind me in the car to get gas." / "When I get in the car, remind me to call my mom." / "Remind me to grab the samples when I'm in the car."
- "Remind me when I park to grab the bag." / "Remind me to grab the bag when I get out of the car."
- "What are my car reminders?" / "Clear my car reminders."

## What Jarvis does
- Handled on the phone (reflex): instant, free, works with no signal. Claude also gets a `remind_in_car` tool (text, out) for freer wording.
- Replies: "I'll remind you in the car, sir." / "I'll remind you next time you're in the car, sir." (already in the car) / "I'll remind you when you park, sir."
- When his car's Bluetooth connects, the usual "Welcome back, sir. Roads look clear." gets the reminders added: "… Reminder: call your mom." (two or more: "Reminders: get gas, and call your mom.")
- When it drops: "Driving mode off, sir. Reminder: grab the bag." With park reminders waiting, this line waits 15 seconds and is skipped if the car reconnects (a Bluetooth blip doesn't use them up).
- A cut-off phrase ("remind me when I get in the car to") saves nothing; car plus a time ("in the car in an hour …", "tomorrow", "at 5", "tonight", "this evening") goes to Claude; "when I park at home to …" keeps just the task.
- Each reminder is said once, then gone. Turned round to "you" ("my mom" → "your mom"), at most 15 words each.
- No car Bluetooth name saved → "Save your car's Bluetooth name in Jarvis first, sir." and nothing is saved.
- Limits: 5 per side (oldest dropped), same reminder not doubled, anything older than 7 days dropped unsaid.
- Not car reminders (go on as before): "remind me to put gas in the car", "remind me in the car" (no task), "remind me when I get home …", "remind me in an hour …", "remind me to clean the car tomorrow".
- Never sends, calls, taps or posts. Kept on the phone.

## Acceptance tests
1. Each "get in" phrasing above (plus "next time I'm in the car remind me to get gas", "remind me once I get into my car to get gas", with "Jarvis", "could you", "please", "sir") → in-car reminder "get gas".
2. Each "get out" phrasing ("when I park", "when I get out of the car", "when I leave the car", "once I park") → out-of-car reminder.
3. The "not car reminders" list → not handled.
4. Two in-car reminders + one park reminder → on connect " Reminders: call your mom, and get gas."; a second connect adds nothing; on disconnect " Reminder: grab the bag."
5. Adding while already in the car → "I'll remind you next time you're in the car, sir."
6. No car name → refusal line, nothing saved.
7. "What are my car reminders?" → "In the car: get gas. When you park: grab your bag." / none → "No car reminders, sir."; "clear my car reminders" → "Cleared 2 car reminders, sir."
8. 6th on one side drops the oldest; duplicates not doubled; 8-day-old reminder dropped unsaid; storage survives a save/load round trip with newlines and a damaged line.
9. A 20-word reminder is spoken as its first 15 words.
10. L8, L17, L20 tests all still pass; the Welcome back / Driving mode off lines are unchanged when nothing's due.
