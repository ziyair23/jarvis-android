# L20 — Say that again

## What he does
- Jarvis says something (an answer, a message read out, a reminder, a call announcement) and he misses it: "Jarvis, say that again." / "What was that?" / "Come again?" / "Repeat that." / "Sorry, what?" / "I didn't catch that." / "One more time."
- Only the end: "Say the last bit again." / "What was the last part?"

## What Jarvis does
- Replays his last spoken line word for word, instantly, on the phone (reflex): no Claude, free, works with no signal.
- "Last bit" replays only the last sentence (the whole line if it's one sentence).
- Nothing spoken in the last 10 minutes (or after "drop it") → "Nothing to repeat, sir."
- Lines from a ringing call or a message drop their question ("Answer it?", "Decline it?", "Want to reply, sir?") when replayed, so a replay never acts on an old call or message.
- A replayed line that carried a call note (L17) stays private: phone voice only, never a cloud voice or the Nest Hub, and it is never added to the conversation Claude sees.
- The replay itself is not recorded as the new "last line", so "say the last bit" then "say all of it again" still has the whole line.
- Quick acknowledgements ("Yes, sir?", "Still on it, sir.") and goodbyes are not recorded; quiet-mode notifications are not spoken, so not recorded.
- Never sends, calls, taps or posts anything.

## Acceptance tests
1. Last line "It's 72 and clear in Columbus, sir. Rain after nine." 1 minute ago → "say that again" → exactly that line.
2. Same → "say the last bit again" → "Rain after nine."; then "say that again" → the whole line still.
3. Phrasings all match: "Jarvis, say that again, sir.", "what was that", "come again", "repeat that", "sorry what", "pardon", "i didn't catch that", "one more time", "what did you say". Not matched: "what did she say", "play it again", "say hello to Abbey", "repeat my last order".
4. 11 minutes after the last line → "Nothing to repeat, sir." Nothing ever said → same.
5. Last line "Abbey is calling, sir. Your note: she owes you twenty. Answer it?" (private) → replay is "Abbey is calling, sir. Your note: she owes you twenty." and `CallNotes.isPrivate(replay)` is true.
6. "Drop it" forgets the last line → "say that again" → "Nothing to repeat, sir."
7. Blank lines are not recorded; a replay line arriving at `said()` doesn't overwrite the stored line.
8. All earlier tests (L8, L17) still pass.
